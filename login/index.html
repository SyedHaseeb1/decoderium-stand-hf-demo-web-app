STAND-HF Clinical Registry Support

For clinical data security, user accounts in the STAND-HF Electronic CRF Portal are provisioned and managed centrally by the Study Administration.

info To request a temporary password or account reactivation, contact your medical centre's Study Coordinator or email the Central Team at support@standhf.pk.

An authorized administrator will issue a temporary one-time password to your registered official contact.
